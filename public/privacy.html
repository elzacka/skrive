Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Summary

Skrive does not collect, store, or share any personal data. Everything you write stays encrypted on your device.

What is stored

  • Notes and folders: Stored locally in your browser, encrypted with AEGIS-256.
  • Settings: Language and display preferences are stored locally.
  • Auto-backup: If enabled, an encrypted copy of your notes is saved to a local folder you choose. The copy is protected with a passphrase you choose, and the file never leaves your device.

What does not happen

  • No data is sent to servers or third parties.
  • No cookies are used.
  • No analytics or tracking tools are used.
  • No user accounts or login required.

Encryption

All notes are encrypted with AEGIS-256 (or XChaCha20-Poly1305 as fallback) before your browser stores them. The encryption key is generated and stored locally on your device.

Your rights

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you have full control. You can:

  • Export all data using the Export function. Files you export yourself are unencrypted, and you choose where they are stored.
  • Delete all data by clearing your browser's storage. If you have enabled auto-backup, also delete the backup file in the folder you chose.
  • Use the app without an internet connection.
